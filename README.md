## The Problem
Students who commute home late, especially girls, face harassment from drivers.
In an emergency, they struggle to share their location and ride details quickly.

## Our Solution
Tadaima helps students prepare before a ride and reach help in one tap:
- Ride log (vehicle and plate number)
- Trusted contacts and a one-tap SOS with a pre-filled SMS
- "Tadaima" button to tell family you're home safe
- Safe places list and safety tips
